# Service page: message and type hierarchy

The first screen now states the offering: AI and systems to redesign everyday work. The supporting sentence names manual work, customer management and web production. A primary link leads to the issue-based service selector; a secondary link leads to contact.

## Type roles

- Page title: 40–56 px desktop, 36 px mobile.
- Section and service headings: 32 px desktop, 28 px mobile.
- Body, buttons and process step titles: 16 px.
- Supplementary labels and service categories: 14 px.

Decorative English headings, the oversized closing signature, and the abstract slider console were removed. The sculpture starts paused and is supplementary on desktop; it is hidden on mobile. The reading order is offering, client problem and support, process, FAQ, contact.
